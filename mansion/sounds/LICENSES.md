# Sound licences

All recordings are CC0 (public domain). Sounds without a file here (murmur, match, sizzle, crackle) are synthesized at startup from the recipes in `engine/Hearth.View/SoundSynth.cs` (the Godot addon's `SoundBank` and the web's player). The web version serves these files as they are and AAC copies of them for Safari (`games/mansion/Mansion.Web/sounds/`, made by `Mansion.Author.Mansion web-sounds`, with their own `LICENSES.md`).
